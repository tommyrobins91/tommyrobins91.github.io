NYU Schack REIT Fund, digital infrastructure and data center sector (2026)

Coverage

I cover the fund's digital infrastructure names: American Tower (AMT, the fund's primary holding), Equinix (EQIX) and Digital Realty (DLR), with comps against Crown Castle (CCI) and SBA Communications (SBAC).

Work

  • Equinix valuation. Built EQIX models on four methods: NAV, FFO/AFFO multiples, a dividend discount model and a DCF. My EQIX pitch was approved by the Investment Committee in October 2026.
  • AMT pre-earnings framework. Positioning call ahead of Q1 2026 results (hold into the print, add at or below $170), with peer comps. AMT beat that quarter.
  • Sector presentations. April 2026 sector update reviewing holdings, and an October 2026 IC presentation covering the sector, AMT, DLR and EQIX balance sheets and rate sensitivity.
  • Sector report. Co-authored a written report on digital infrastructure and data centers.
  • Portfolio reduction. Contributed to cutting the fund from 20 holdings to 12, including disposition cases for weaker positions outside my sector.

Themes

AI-driven data center demand flowing into colocation leasing, tower densification, and how rising rates hit long-duration infrastructure REITs.
