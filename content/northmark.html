What it is

Northmark Intelligence is a Toronto-based research venture I'm building. The idea is to use AI to produce Canadian commercial real estate research for private equity clients, covering markets that the large research shops cover thinly or not at all.

Work so far

  • GTA CRE market brief. Market-level research on the Greater Toronto Area across asset classes.
  • AI adoption strategy deck. An 18-slide presentation for a private equity real estate firm on where AI fits in deal sourcing and asset management, with a data privacy framework. It doesn't push any one vendor.
  • Investor pitch. A 9-slide deck on AI in real estate, prepared for a Toronto real estate investment manager.
  • Guelph residential comp tracker. Sold low-rise listings pulled into an Excel tracker with list-to-sale ratios, days on market, building age and a link to each listing.
  • Muskoka Development Pipeline. The first Northmark data product. See the case study.

Product ideas tested

CAM reconciliation as a niche AI product, and a sustainability reporting platform. I researched both and neither moved past that stage. Knowing when to drop an idea is part of the work.
